Add the PER Global Summary and Global Performance dashboards to the Preparedness page

- Global Summary: a map of the National Societies coloured by the phase of their current PER cycle, KPIs for the PER phases and completed cycles, the assessment types, the PER processes by year and region, the High Priority Components and the PER considerations
- Filter the Global Summary by region, year, phase, assessment type, High Priority Component, consideration or National Society, and export the filtered data to Excel
- Global Performance: the PER cycles with the number of National Societies and the average PER rating, and the ratings of the PER areas and components, including each sub-component of component 14
- Filter the Global Performance by region and cycle
- The dashboards load their data from the PER API, are available in English, French, Spanish and Arabic, use the colours of the other PER pages and can be used with the keyboard
